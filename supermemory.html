Why we built this

Rereading a semester's worth of notes before an exam is slow, and it's easy to zone out halfway down the page. Most of what's in there is a definition you already half-know — what's missing is seeing it actually work.

Supermemory compresses long notes into short summaries, one topic at a time, and pairs each one with a real, working example. Less rereading, more sticking.

— built by Sanuthi, Pahandi & Binara, for our own classes.
